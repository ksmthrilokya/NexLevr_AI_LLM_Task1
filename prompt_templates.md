# Prompt Engineering Templates for Marketing Copy
## Nexlevr AI & LLM Fundamentals Internship - Beginner Task 1

---

### Template 1: Instagram Caption
**Prompt:**
"Write an Instagram caption for [PRODUCT/SERVICE] targeting [AUDIENCE], in a [TONE] tone, including 3 relevant hashtags and a call-to-action."

**Example (filled in):**
"Write an Instagram caption for a handmade jewelry brand targeting young women aged 18-25, in a fun and trendy tone, including 3 relevant hashtags and a call-to-action."

---

### Template 2: Email Subject Line
**Prompt:**
"Generate 5 email subject lines for a [CAMPAIGN TYPE] email promoting [PRODUCT/OFFER], aimed at [AUDIENCE]. Keep each under 60 characters and make them attention-grabbing."

**Example (filled in):**
"Generate 5 email subject lines for a flash sale email promoting 30% off summer clothing, aimed at existing customers. Keep each under 60 characters and make them attention-grabbing."

---

### Template 3: Product Description
**Prompt:**
"Write a compelling product description for [PRODUCT NAME], highlighting its [KEY FEATURES], for an e-commerce website. Keep it under 100 words and focus on the benefits to the customer."

**Example (filled in):**
"Write a compelling product description for a stainless steel water bottle, highlighting its insulation and eco-friendly design, for an e-commerce website. Keep it under 100 words and focus on the benefits to the customer."

---

### Template 4: Advertisement Copy
**Prompt:**
"Create a short advertisement copy (2-3 sentences) for [BUSINESS/PRODUCT] to run on [PLATFORM], targeting [AUDIENCE]. The tone should be [TONE] and it must include a clear offer or benefit."

**Example (filled in):**
"Create a short advertisement copy (2-3 sentences) for a local bakery to run on Facebook, targeting families in the neighborhood. The tone should be warm and inviting and it must include a clear offer or benefit."

---

### Template 5: Blog Post Title
**Prompt:**
"Suggest 5 catchy blog post titles about [TOPIC] for an audience interested in [NICHE]. Make the titles SEO-friendly and click-worthy."

**Example (filled in):**
"Suggest 5 catchy blog post titles about time management tips for an audience interested in productivity for college students. Make the titles SEO-friendly and click-worthy."
